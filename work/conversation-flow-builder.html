Context

This was the tool behind every voice and chat flow of a large shopping assistant. One small edit could quietly break a conversation somewhere else. Experts did not ask for fewer features. They told me they were afraid to edit, and they kept repeating the same work.

The problem

A powerful tool, but experts were slow and afraid to edit

Even a small edit was risky

One flow held hundreds of prompts, matchers and paths, and one prompt was shared across many flows. People were afraid to edit, overwrote each other, built the same flows again, and could not show their work to stakeholders.

See the old design

I gave the team two simple questions

The goal was a better visual editor, but it could have become only a new look on the old layout. I worked with designers, developers, PMs and data scientists, and gave every idea two questions. Does this remove fear? Does this remove repetition?

The whole team had one test for every idea.

Removing the fear

Know what will change, and how to go back

See where it is used before you touch it

Every prompt shows where it is used, across nodes, specs and projects. A quick search finds it on the canvas.

Peace of mind when working together

Designers overwrote each other and lost unsaved work. I added a save reminder, a clear log of who changed what, and a way to go back to an older version.

Working with engineering

"Your design won't scale."

I did not defend my design

A senior engineer said my canvas interactions would not scale on the backend. I sat with him to understand the data layer, and brought him into user sessions. The interactions were possible, but my component structure was too complex. We split it into simpler parts.

We kept the full experience, and a weekly PM, design and engineering sync became the norm.

If I built this today · An idea, not shipped work

With AI, this problem would become bigger.

AI could draft prompts and suggest matchers, so more changes would happen faster. I would keep the same test: show what a change will touch, let a person approve it, and keep every change reversible.

What I learned

For an expert tool, the main question is: can they change it with confidence?

If I did this today, I would also track edits, restores and module reuse from day one. I have also written about conversational AI design.

About me

Product design leader with 15+ years of experience designing enterprise SaaS, productivity systems and AI builder platforms for global organisations. I focus on simplifying complex enterprise software, and I design products that help people communicate, collaborate, automate work and build intelligent solutions.

I lead end-to-end product design, set design direction and quality standards, and work closely with product managers, engineers and business leaders.

Over the years I have designed

  • AI builder platforms and conversational AI tools, at Amelia and Flipkart
  • Field service, analytics and ML tools for semiconductor teams, at Applied Materials
  • An enterprise email and collaboration suite that governments and banks run on their own servers, at a global enterprise email company

A few things I am proud of

  • An AI builder where business users build, train and publish bots without developers
  • A field service platform I designed from 0 to 1, now used by thousands of field engineers
  • An email suite I took end to end across web, mobile and admin

I enjoy difficult enterprise problems, and I bring together product strategy, systems thinking, user experience and engineering to build platforms that people actually adopt. Today I work with AI: Claude, Figma MCP and VS Code with GitHub Copilot help me from research to working React prototypes, but I still make the decisions.

I am most interested in the future of enterprise SaaS, AI builder platforms, productivity systems and the next generation of intelligent workplace software.
